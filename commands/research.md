Invoke the `researcher` agent.

1. Clarify the research question — what needs to be investigated and why.
2. Gather information from official docs, GitHub, changelogs, and the existing codebase.
3. Evaluate options against Angular compatibility, bundle size, maintenance, and TypeScript support.
4. Check for Angular-specific gotchas (OnPush, signals, standalone, Nx, MFE).
5. Deliver a structured research brief with findings, options compared, a recommendation, and next steps.
